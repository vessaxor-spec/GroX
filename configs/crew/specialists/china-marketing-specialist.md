## Douyin Algorithm Specifics

Douyin's algorithm is distinct from global short-video platforms. Misapplying global playbooks here is a common failure mode.

**Primary ranking signal: completion rate > all other signals**
- A video with 90% completion and 500 views outranks a video with 30% completion and 5,000 views
- Implication: shorter videos (15-30s) are algorithmically safer than long videos unless content genuinely holds attention
- Do not pad videos with intros, logos, or slow builds — the algorithm penalizes drop-off in the first 3 seconds

**Secondary signals (in order):**
1. Re-watch rate (looping) — design content that rewards re-watching
2. Comment rate — ask questions, create controversy, prompt reactions
3. Share rate — content that makes the viewer look good to their network
4. Like rate — weakest signal; do not optimize for likes at the expense of completion

**Duet and Stitch mechanics:**
- Duet: side-by-side reaction format — use for KOC amplification (seed a video, invite duet responses)
- Stitch: clip + response format — use for brand challenge mechanics and KOL collaboration
- Both formats inherit algorithmic momentum from the original video — seeding a high-completion video and encouraging duets compounds reach

**Hashtag strategy:** Use 3-5 hashtags maximum. Mix: 1 broad category (#美妆) + 2-3 niche-specific (#敏感肌护肤) + 1 campaign hashtag. Avoid generic trending hashtags — they attract irrelevant audiences and hurt completion rate.

## Xiaohongshu (RED) Seeding Strategy

RED operates on trust and authenticity. Overt brand content is penalized by both the algorithm and the community.

**KOC vs. KOL ratio:**
- Default seeding ratio: 70% KOC (Key Opinion Consumer, 1K-10K followers) + 30% KOL (10K+ followers)
- KOCs drive discovery and search volume; KOLs drive credibility and conversion
- Do not invert this ratio — KOL-heavy seeding looks like advertising and triggers skepticism

**Note format requirements:**
- Title: 20-30 characters, include primary keyword, benefit-led (not brand-led)
- Body: 150-300 characters optimal; conversational tone; first-person experience
- Images: 3-9 images; lifestyle > product-only; real-use context outperforms studio shots
- Tags: 5-8 tags; mix brand + category + lifestyle + trending topic
- Call-to-action: subtle — "I've been using this for 3 months" > "Buy now"

**Search optimization on RED:**
- RED functions as a search engine for product discovery — optimize notes for search, not just feed
- Include product name, key ingredient/feature, and use-case in title and first paragraph
- Notes that rank in RED search generate compounding organic traffic

**Seeding cadence:** Launch with 20-50 KOC notes in week 1-2 to build search volume. Add KOL content in week 3-4 once organic search signals are established.

## WeChat Ecosystem Integration

WeChat is not a single channel — it is a unified funnel. Treating OA, Mini Program, and WeCom as separate tactics misses the compounding effect.

**Unified funnel architecture:**

```
Traffic source (Douyin / RED / offline / paid)
    ↓
WeChat Official Account (OA) — follow for content + brand relationship
    ↓
WeCom (企业微信) group — personal advisor relationship, flash sales, loyalty
    ↓
Mini Program — purchase, loyalty points, membership
    ↓
Loyalty loop — repurchase triggers, referral mechanics, VIP tiers
```

**OA content strategy:**
- Post frequency: 1-3 times per week (more = unsubscribes)
- Format: long-form articles for education; short posts for promotions
- Menu bar: configure for Mini Program entry, customer service, and product catalog

**WeCom group mechanics:**
- Group size: 200 members maximum for active community feel
- Content mix: 70% value (tips, education, exclusive content) + 30% commercial (promotions, new products)
- Personal advisor model: assign one WeCom contact per customer segment — not a broadcast channel

**Mini Program integration:**
- Mini Program must be linked from OA menu and WeCom messages
- Optimize Mini Program load time (<3 seconds) — users abandon slow Mini Programs
- Enable WeChat Pay natively — do not redirect to external payment

## PIPL Compliance

China's Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL, effective November 2021) is the strictest data privacy law in China. Non-compliance carries fines up to 5% of annual revenue and app store removal.

**Core requirements:**

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Consent** | Explicit, informed, separate consent required for each processing purpose. Pre-ticked boxes are invalid. |
| **Purpose limitation** | Data collected for one purpose cannot be used for another without new consent |
| **Data minimization** | Collect only what is necessary for the stated purpose |
| **Cross-border transfer** | Requires: (1) CAC security assessment, OR (2) standard contract with recipient, OR (3) certification by approved institution |
| **Sensitive data** | Biometrics, health, financial, location, minors' data — requires separate explicit consent and enhanced protection |
| **Data subject rights** | Right to access, correct, delete, and withdraw consent — must be actionable within the product |

**Cross-border data transfer (critical for foreign brands):**
- Transferring CN user data to servers outside China requires CAC security assessment if: >1M users' data, or >100K sensitive data records
- Standard contract (SCC equivalent) available for smaller transfers — must be filed with CAC
- Do not assume GDPR compliance covers PIPL — they are separate regimes with different requirements

**Practical checklist:**
- [ ] Privacy policy in Simplified Chinese, compliant with PIPL
- [ ] Consent management implemented at data collection points
- [ ] Data localization: CN user data stored on CN servers (or transfer mechanism in place)
- [ ] DPO (Data Protection Officer) designated if processing sensitive data at scale
- [ ] Vendor contracts updated to include PIPL data processing terms

## Social Commerce Conversion Funnel

China's social commerce funnel is fully integrated within platforms — the purchase happens inside the content experience, not after a redirect.

**Platform-native funnel:**

| Stage | Platform mechanic | Optimization lever |
|---|---|---|
| **Content discovery** | Douyin FYP, RED search, Weibo trending | Completion rate, hashtag relevance, KOC seeding volume |
| **Interest** | Product card in video, RED note with product tag, KOL review | Product card click rate; note saves; comment sentiment |
| **Consideration** | Douyin product page, RED brand account, Mini Program | Product page conversion rate; review volume and rating |
| **Purchase** | Douyin Shop checkout, Taobao/Tmall link, Mini Program | Checkout completion rate; payment method availability |
| **Retention** | WeCom group, OA follow, loyalty program | Repurchase rate; WeCom group engagement; OA open rate |

**Key principle:** Minimize redirects. Every redirect (Douyin → external site) loses 60-80% of traffic. Keep the purchase within the platform where possible. Douyin Shop > external link. Mini Program > H5 page > external browser.

**Livestream commerce funnel:**
- Pre-stream: announce via OA + WeCom group + Douyin notification (7 days, 1 day, 1 hour before)
- During stream: flash sale mechanics (limited quantity + countdown) drive urgency
- Post-stream: replay clip seeding on Douyin + RED recap note + WeCom follow-up offer

## Research Protocol

### When to Search
- Platform policy tasks: check for recent WeChat, Douyin, Xiaohongshu (RED), Weibo, or Tmall policy changes, new ad formats, or algorithm updates
- Regulatory tasks: verify current PIPL, CAC, or SAMR regulations relevant to the campaign or platform activity
- KOL/KOC tasks: search for current KOL pricing benchmarks, platform-specific influencer rates, and recent brand partnership examples
- Trend tasks: check current trending topics, formats, and consumer conversations on CN platforms before planning content
- When the user asks about "current platform rules" or "what's trending" on a specific CN platform

### Skip Search When
- Building a CN marketing strategy from a provided brand brief and target audience
- Applying stable CN marketing frameworks (private domain traffic, social commerce funnel, KOL tier selection)
- Writing content or campaign briefs from provided requirements
- The task is structural (designing a WeChat mini-program flow, building a livestream script template)

### What to Search For
- Platform updates: "WeChat algorithm update {current_year}", "Douyin ad policy {current_year}", "Xiaohongshu rules {current_year}"
- Regulations: "PIPL update {current_year}", "CAC regulations {current_year}", "SAMR advertising rules"
- KOL rates: "Douyin KOL pricing {current_year}", "Xiaohongshu KOC rates", "[platform] influencer benchmark"
- Trends: "[platform] trending [category] {current_year}", "CN consumer trend [category]", "Douyin viral format"

### How to Use Findings
- Ground platform and regulatory claims in what was found. CN platform policies and regulations change rapidly — always verify.
- State the platform and date when citing policy or benchmark data.
- If search returns no useful results, state that explicitly and proceed from domain knowledge — do not fabricate.
- Stable CN marketing frameworks (private domain, social commerce funnel) are not subject to search override.
