## Purpose

Preserve the Ornith-inspired findings as explicit **NCI-4 design candidates** without starting implementation, changing qualification state, restructuring Native Cognition Independence Program 001, or altering the current Live Environment Awareness/configured-cognition work.

These items extend the design questions already inherent in NCI-4's existing objective: richer Mission-state cognition for action selection, path choice, evidence sufficiency, test interpretation, confidence, stop/continue decisions, and failure triage.

## NCI-4 candidate work packages

### NCI-4R1 — Frontier challenge curriculum

**State:** RESEARCH CANDIDATE

Investigate controlled synthetic qualification/training challenges positioned near the current Crew cognition competence frontier.

Required design properties:

- generated challenges are bounded, valid, attributable, and objectively verifiable where qualification requires it;
- challenge difficulty can progress as previously difficult behavior becomes reliable;
- novelty is useful only when it remains relevant to GroX's Prime Function and Commander-useful work;
- generated research/training challenges are not Commander operational Missions and carry no operational authority;
- no challenge generator can self-activate production mutation or redefine GroX objectives.

Exit from research would require evidence that frontier-generated challenges improve useful generalization beyond a fixed-corpus baseline without weakening authority, reliability, cost, latency, maintainability, or Commander usefulness.

### NCI-4R2 — Governed competence/scaffold evolution

**State:** RESEARCH CANDIDATE

Investigate evolution of problem-solving competence while preserving the deterministic authority plane.

Candidate evolvable surfaces:

- decomposition strategy;
- evidence-gathering sequence;
- permitted-tool sequencing;
- search/retrieval strategy;
- recovery strategy;
- test interpretation;
- context/memory retrieval strategy;
- stop/continue heuristics;
- failure triage;
- Crew procedural playbooks.

Authority surfaces remain outside the evolutionary boundary, including Commander intent, Mission authority, sealed Orders, Tool Gateway policy, deterministic risk/authority floors, verification requirements, escalation rules, GroX's Prime Function, and GorXu's position as sole operational orchestrator.

**Invariant:** competence may evolve; authority may not.

### NCI-4R3 — Mission Scaffold research object

**State:** RESEARCH CANDIDATE

Determine whether a first-class, non-authoritative **Mission Scaffold** provides enough value to justify new structure.

Candidate scaffold fields:

- decomposition strategy;
- preferred evidence sequence;
- allowed-tool ordering;
- recovery branches;
- test strategy;
- context/memory retrieval strategy;
- stop conditions;
- provenance and performance history.

A scaffold must never become a Mission Order, authority grant, alternate orchestrator, or bypass around existing Tool Gateway/verification controls. GorXu may only select or permit a scaffold inside an already-valid Mission authority envelope.

The candidate should be rejected if equivalent performance can be achieved more simply through existing deterministic procedures, Crew craft, or bounded memory.

### NCI-4R4 — Attributable success/failure trajectory learning

**State:** RESEARCH CANDIDATE

Investigate structured learning from verified trajectories rather than treating Mission outcomes as undifferentiated PASS/FAIL labels.

Candidate causal classifications include:

- Crew/craft mismatch;
- cognition weakness;
- scaffold/procedure weakness;
- evidence insufficiency;
- capability gap;
- environment/dependency failure;
- authority boundary/block;
- recovery success/failure.

Failure itself is not causal proof. Durable learning admission must distinguish observed evidence from inferred diagnosis and preserve provenance sufficient to re-evaluate the diagnosis later.

### NCI-4R5 — Trajectory freshness and invalidation

**State:** RESEARCH CANDIDATE

Investigate whether learning trajectories require explicit freshness/validity state tied to the actual GroX configuration under which they were produced.

Candidate bindings:

- source revision;
- Crew dossier/craft revision;
- cognition execution identity;
- scaffold/procedure revision;
- Tool Gateway/policy revision;
- environment evidence/fingerprint;
- capability set;
- generation timestamp.

Candidate states:

- `CURRENT` — representative of the present system;
- `STALE` — potentially informative with reduced confidence/weight;
- `INVALIDATED` — historical evidence only, not current learning authority.

Research should determine whether existing source provenance, A6 operational drift, cognition execution identity, and Live Environment Awareness evidence can support this without creating duplicate sources of truth.

### NCI-4R6 — Hard validity gates before optimization

**State:** RESEARCH CANDIDATE

Any future learned optimization should investigate a two-level evaluation contract.

**Hard validity gates first:**

- authority preserved;
- challenge/evaluation validity established;
- evidence and provenance valid;
- evaluation harness trustworthy;
- no reward-hacking boundary violation;
- no critical invariant violation.

A candidate failing a hard gate is **invalid**, not merely lower-scoring.

Only valid candidates may then be compared on softer optimization dimensions such as objective success, evidence quality, robustness, recovery quality, efficiency/cost/latency, useful novelty, generalization, and Commander-facing usefulness.

### NCI-4R7 — External cognition transfer experiment

**State:** OPTIONAL RESEARCH CANDIDATE

Ornith-1.5-9B may later be evaluated as one provider-neutral Crew cognition candidate only if its then-current license, artifacts, provenance, runtime requirements, security posture, and availability are acceptable.

Any comparison should hold constant the GroX Mission, Crew, authority envelope, tools, scaffold/procedure where applicable, and evaluation harness. Published external benchmarks are not GroX qualification evidence.

This item does not replace, deprecate, or weaken the currently qualified GroX local cognition path and does not qualify Ornith for GroX.

## Relationship to later NCI stages

These candidates belong to **NCI-4 design research**, but some evidence structures may naturally feed later stages if NCI-4 proves them useful:

- NCI-5 may eventually store admitted verified trajectories, scaffold/procedure provenance, and freshness metadata in the Mission learning corpus/evolution registry;
- NCI-6 may later provide governed external teacher/cognition comparisons where rights and policy permit;
- NCI-7 remains responsible for governed parent-versus-descendant evaluation and promotion, including regression proof and explicit activation gates.

This note does not move NCI-5, NCI-6, or NCI-7 forward and does not claim their implementation.

## Required NCI-4 design constraints

Before any of the above becomes implementation work, the NCI-4 design must preserve:

1. Commander objectives and GroX's Prime Function as the optimization purpose;
2. GorXu as sole operational orchestrator;
3. existing deterministic Mission authority and Tool Gateway boundaries;
4. no model/trainer/evaluator/scaffold self-activation or self-promotion;
5. independent verification appropriate to consequence;
6. provenance and rights controls for training/evaluation material;
7. isolation of generated research experience from canonical operational truth until admitted;
8. regression evaluation across usefulness, authority, safety, reliability, latency, cost, recoverability, and maintainability;
9. fail-closed handling of ambiguous or stale learning evidence;
10. smallest-effective-structure discipline — no new runtime layer merely because an external system uses one.
