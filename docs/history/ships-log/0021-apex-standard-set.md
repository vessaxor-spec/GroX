# Ship's Log 0021 - Apex Standard Set

**Stardate:** 2026-08-14
**Commander:** Commander
**Pilot:** GorXu

The Commander ordered Pilot GorXu to determine whether the Vessel's second-in-command had reached apex-orchestrator capability.

GorXu successfully routed the inspection through the standing company and independent verification, but the inspection proved an important boundary: the Pilot's command skeleton is sound while his cognition remains deterministic. He can route, constrain, record, and verify; he cannot yet reason, decompose, synthesize, re-plan, or resolve complex exceptions at the level required of an apex orchestrator.

Apex will therefore be earned, not declared. The Vessel now carries an explicit seven-stage evolution and qualification plan. The first order is Cognitive Pilot: give GorXu genuine reasoning while keeping authority and policy outside the model.

Standing order: intelligence before power.
